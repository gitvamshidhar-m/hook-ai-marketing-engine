## Tips for the real CV

1. Put a clear **Digital Marketing** title at the top.
2. Start bullets with **metrics** ("Grew SEO footprint to [N] pages", "+[N]%", "[N] subscribers").
3. Put the ** live link** right under your name.
4. Keep it to **one page** — pick your 6 strongest bullets.
5. On GitHub, link the README and this case study.
