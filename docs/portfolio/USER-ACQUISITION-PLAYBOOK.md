# Get Your First 30–100 Users (and the Metrics to Show)

Your #1 gap as a digital marketing candidate is **proof of reach**. You don't need thousands of
users — you need believable, honest numbers: real signups, real emails, real Google visits. This
playbook gets you there in about 2–3 weeks of small, consistent effort.

---

## Goal (reusable evidence)

| Metric | Target | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Email subscribers | 50–150 | `/api/captures` table |
| User signups | 25–60 | `/growth` → Signups |
| Organic visits | 200+ | Google Search Console |
| Campaign runs / leads | 50+ | `/growth` |
| Indexed pages | 32+ | Search Console |

Even **10–20 real users** is enough to make the product feel alive. Screenshots from `/growth` and
`/analytics` are your proof.

---

## Phase 1 — Build the email list (fast wins, days 1–3)

1. Post the live tool where marketers already hang out:
   - Reddit: r/Entrepreneur, r/marketing, r/smallbusiness. Many subs allow "I built a free tool" if
     you are genuine and not spammy.
   - X / Threads / Twitter: "I built a free AI hook generator. Try it: [link]"
   - Indie Hackers and Product Hunt "build in public" threads.
2. The exit popup and the "share to earn" flow already capture emails — let them work.
3. Keep it free and frictionless: no credit card, instant try.

## Phase 2 — Turn tries into signups

1. The tool is free to try — that is the top of the funnel.
2. After someone generates hooks, invite them with: "Save it to your account." That is the signup
   hook.
3. Let people share a saved campaign — it looks great and is your peer visual proof.

## Phase 3 — Organic traffic (do a little, rank more)

1. Your 32 posts are already written and in the sitemap. Push them: share a few on LinkedIn and
   Reddit once, keep them linked internally.
2. Add one "versus" or "best tools round-up" post — the type other sites link to (link bait).
3. Turn `/community` into a weekly "top hooks" post people like to share.

## Phase 4 — Record the proof everywhere

1. Screenshot `/growth` and `/analytics` each week.
2. Note Search Console index count and clicks.
3. Drop those numbers into the placeholders in `CASE-STUDY.md` and the resume bullets.

---

## If you only have one day

1. Post the tool link in 2–3 communities.
2. Ask a few friends to create accounts and run 2–3 campaigns each.
3. Screenshot `/growth` and `/analytics`, then add the numbers to the case study.

That alone turns your CV from "a project" into "a product with users."

---

## Guard the weakness

- Never fake numbers. Recruiters verify — real-but-small beats fake.
- The "0 signups" counter on the home page is the single most visible gap. A handful of real users
  removes it.
